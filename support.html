Support

How can we help?

For help with Dawnify, email support@dawnify.app.

Alarms and permissions

Check that Dawnify has the notification and alarm permissions needed for the feature you are using. You can review permissions in Dawnify’s Settings and in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions, trials, and refunds

Dawnify subscriptions and free trials are managed by Apple. To view, change, or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, then tap Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases in Dawnify if an existing purchase is not recognised.

Contact support

When you email us, include your device model, iOS version, Dawnify version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Privacy

For privacy questions or data requests, email support@dawnify.app or read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
